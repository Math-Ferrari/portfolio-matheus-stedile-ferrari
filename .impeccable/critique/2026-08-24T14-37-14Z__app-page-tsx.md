## Design Specificity Verdict

The portfolio is clearly authored. Floating Lines, the mineral palette, editorial typography, natural portrait and real operational-product screenshots form a recognizable identity. Hero, Projects and Contact have their own character. Specificity drops in the middle: How I Work and Stack/Engineering rely on neutral typographic patterns that could belong to another software portfolio with little change.

The deterministic scan returned zero findings for `app/page.tsx`; this is a clean result for the compositor, not a recursive clean bill for imported components. ## Overall Impression

The foundation is sophisticated and professional. The single biggest opportunity is to preserve the authored editorial rhythm after Projects while removing one trust-breaking control and a handful of mobile proportion defects.

## What’s Working

- The desktop Hero is specific, balanced and proportional at 1440×900 and 1920×1080.
- Project modules keep a strong image/content hierarchy from stacked mobile cards through 6/6 tablet and 5/7 desktop layouts.
- Photography and product captures remain natural and un-tinted, fully aligned with DESIGN.md.
- No viewport showed page-level overflow or broken content.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] PT/EN simulates unavailable functionality.** EN receives selected state without translating any content, weakening trust. Replace it with a truthful non-interactive “EN em breve” signal or hide it until localization exists. Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`.
2. **[P2] Mobile continuity and access to Projects are weaker than desktop.** The mobile Hero drops the explicit “Ver projetos” path and uses an isolated internal axis. Preserve the photographic composition, but add a quiet mobile-only route to Projects and keep the text axis intentional. Suggested command: `/impeccable adapt`.
3. **[P2] Projects counter wraps accidentally at 375 px.** `01—03` moves to a second left-aligned line while it fits correctly at 430 px. Use a two-column header grid or a deliberate right-aligned second line. Suggested command: `/impeccable layout`.
4. **[P2] The middle becomes visually generic.** How I Work and Stack/Engineering repeat headings, dividers and list density without carrying the editorial signature established by Hero and Projects. Vary composition with existing neutral tones, rules and editorial axes; do not add cards, shadows or new colors. Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`.
5. **[P3] Mobile finishing details are inconsistent.** Some navigation/footer targets are shorter than the 44 px comfort floor; Contact’s `min-height:70vh` leaves a low-information tail before the footer; the 375 px brand label is very close to truncation. Normalize mobile hit areas and remove the artificial mobile minimum without changing desktop. Suggested command: `/impeccable adapt`.

## Minor Observations

- Muted desktop text is intentionally restrained and should not be reduced further.
- The Footer is coherent but tall on mobile and repeats fixed navigation.
- The final quiet CTA is legible but less energetic than the closing headline.
- The fixed translucent navbar can pass over dense text during scroll; no content becomes unreachable.
